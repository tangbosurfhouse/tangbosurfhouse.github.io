Nourishing Island Food

Breakfast & Dinner Menus

Nutritious, mouth-watering meals crafted with local ingredients. Buffet breakfasts and family dinners cooked fresh daily by our local aunties.

Day 1: Filipino Welcome Feast

Buffet
  • Silog Station: Tapsilog served with garlic fried rice and sunny-side-up eggs
  • Champorado: Warm chocolate rice porridge made with rich local tablea & condensed milk
  • Fresh Fruits: Sliced sweet mangoes, ripe papayas, and bananas
  • Breads & Spreads: Warm Pandesal with butter, peanut butter, and assorted jams

Day 2: Wellness Morning

Healthy Fuel
  • Quinoa & Chia Porridge: Cooked with almond milk, topped with mango & toasted nuts
  • Smoothie Bar: Fresh berry smoothies with banana, garden spinach & island fruits
  • Granola Bowls: Crunchy homemade granola with creamy yogurt & fruits
  • Sourdough Toast: Smashed avocado, poached eggs & grilled island tomatoes
  • Beverages: Fresh coconut water & selection of herbal teas

Day 3: Sweet & Savory Mix

Comfort Food
  • Pancakes & Waffles: Golden stack served with warm syrups and fresh fruits
  • Chia Seed Pudding: Infused with coconut milk & topped with tropical mango
  • Breakfast Muffins: Savory baked muffins packed with spinach, veg & cheese
  • Yogurt Parfaits: Layered with fruit compote and toasted island oats

Day 4: Protein Power-Up

High Energy
  • Egg Station: Cooked to order — scrambled, fried, or poached eggs
  • Meats & Sides: Crisp bacon, breakfast sausages, and ham (optional)
  • Vegetarian Plate: Sliced tomatoes, crisp cucumbers, onions & sautéed greens
  • Toast & Spreads: Whole grain bread with butter and fruit preserves
  • Fruit Basket: Fresh bananas and crunchy sweet apples

Day 5: Breakfast Wrap Buffet

Custom Builds
  • Warm Wraps: Soft tortillas with fluffy scrambled eggs
  • Roast & Greens: Roasted sweet potato cubes, sautéed spinach, diced peppers & onions
  • Cheeses & Toppings: Crumbled feta cheese and shredded sharp cheddar
  • Sauces: Fresh tomato salsa, sour cream, smashed avocado & homemade hummus
  • Fruit Assortment: Platter of chilled island fruits

Day 6 & 7: Camp Choice Encore

Guest Favorite

On Day 6, our team asks the group which breakfast feast they loved the most to recreate an all-time camp favorite!

Day 1: Kinilaw (Filipino Ceviche)

Local Specialty
  • Main: Fresh Yellowfin tuna marinated in coconut vinegar, calamansi, ginger, chili & coconut milk Veg/Vegan: Tofu & Cucumber
  • Side: Ensaladang talong (charred smoky eggplant salad with tomato & onions)
  • Bonus: Steamed garlic rice & freshly cooked island vegetables (chopseuy)
  • Dessert: Handcrafted sweet mango float

Day 2: Artisan Pasta Night

Italian Twist
  • Mains: Trio of fragrant basil pesto pasta, creamy spinach pasta & rich tomato pasta
  • Additions: Grilled fish and marinated chicken breast Veg: Mung bean & veg
  • Side: Crisp garden salad with extra virgin olive oil vinaigrette & shaved cheese
  • Dessert: Freshly baked warm banana bread

Day 3: Island Tortilla Fiesta

Gluten-Free Opt
  • Mains: Soft warm tortilla wraps (gluten-free wraps available on request)
  • Fillings: Sautéed garden vegetables, bell peppers, onions & mushrooms
  • Proteins: Spiced fish & chicken Veg/Vegan: Mung bean or tofu
  • Side: Sweet grilled sweet corn & seasoned yellow rice
  • Dessert: Swedish chocolate oat balls

Day 4: Island Beach BBQ

Charcoal Grill
  • Main: Fresh BBQ catch of the day, marinated meats & grilled vegetable skewers with eggplant, peppers, and tomatoes
  • Sides: Herb roasted potatoes & crunchy island coleslaw
  • Dessert: Sweet chilled watermelon and golden pineapple slices

Day 5: Camp Favorites Encore

Voted by Guests

On Day 5, we ask our guests which dinner dish was their number one highlight, and our aunties cook it fresh for an encore feast!

Day 6: Celebration Dinner

Party Night
  • Main: Fresh artisanal pizzas (vegetarian, fresh fish, and meat lovers options)
  • Sides: Golden fries, marinated olives & mozzarella balls
  • Party: Graduation drinks, cocktails, beers & camp party!
Tangbo Essentials

What We Offer

Whether you're booking an all-inclusive camp or an a la carte stay, here is everything available at Tangbo Surf House.

🏄

Surf Lessons & Coaching

Beginner to intermediate coaching with patient local instructors, board rentals, and ocean theory sessions right at Northern Siargao breaks.

🛵

Motorbike / Bike Rental

Well-maintained automatic scooters and motorbikes equipped with board racks and helmets so you can explore the island at your own pace.

🚐

Airport Transfers

Hassle-free, direct van transfers between Sayak Siargao Airport and Tangbo Surf House so you never have to stress upon arrival.

🍳

Bed & Breakfast

Wake up to the sounds of the ocean and enjoy wholesome buffet-style breakfasts freshly prepared every morning before the first session.

🍽

Homecooked Dinners

Hearty, nourishing group family dinners prepared with love by our local aunties, featuring fresh seafood, Filipino classics, and vegan options.

🧘

Yoga & Movement

Energising morning stretches and restorative evening flows to soothe surf-tired muscles and cultivate mindfulness in our open-air spaces.

🎨

Paint & Sip Nights

Unwind on the terrace with wine, cocktails, brushes, and good music for an artistic, memorable evening under the northern island stars.

🌿

Farm Experience & Cooking Class

Visit the Tropical Academy local farm, harvest organic island produce, and learn how to cook authentic Filipino specialties.

🏝

Island Exploring & Boat Trips

Discover untouched sandbars, hidden cliff dives at Hagukan, snorkeling reefs, and refreshing cold spring pools with our local crew.
