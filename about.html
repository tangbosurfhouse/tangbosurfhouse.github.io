Our Story

The Story Behind Tangbo

Born from a deep love for uncrowded waves, ocean living, and peaceful island days, Tangbo Surf House is your tranquil sanctuary in the untouched north of Siargao.

Tangbo Surf House

Sapao, Siargao Island

Tangbo Surf House is located along the serene northern coastline of Santa Monica (Sapao), Siargao Island. Nestled along the circumferential road with direct beachfront access, our home offers a peaceful sanctuary far from crowded tourist spots. Wake up to panoramic ocean views, fresh sea breezes, and authentic island tranquility.

Uncrowded Waves & Island Life

Northern Coast Lineups

Experience Siargao surfing the way it was meant to be. Located right near legendary northern reef breaks including the famous Tangbo A-Frame and Pacific swells, you will find clean, uncrowded lineups for all skill levels. Beyond the waves, enjoy scenic coastal drives, quiet palm-fringed shores, and warm island hospitality.
